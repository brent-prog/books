# Crucial Conversations — source-fidelity audit

**Audit basis:** `Crucial Conversations Summary.pdf` (all four illustrated pages), the associated Notion Markdown export and images, and the `crucial-conversations/index.html` replacement on branch `design/crucial-conversations-editorial-rebuild`.

**Editorial hierarchy:** the four-page illustrated summary controls the main page's sequence and priority; the Notion notes provide depth, qualifications, examples and exception handling in the explanatory pop-outs. Do not mistake the order of Notion chapters for the user's visual presentation.

**Design rule:** the hand-drawn Middle Way figure is a conceptual anchor, not old artwork to copy. The red identity and the concepts in the existing illustrations are to be elevated with modern, purposeful visual design. Do not turn the page into a graphic novel, stock-icon set, or a copy of the Zero to One page.

## Expanded detail from the Notion export

- **40 original detailed concepts** are retained in the preview's source-data inventory. Their pop-outs now provide separate principle, explanation/why it matters, practical application, and a concrete example/question.
- **9 additional foundational pop-outs** include the Middle Way, three conversation choices, monkey-brain stress, the Law, Silence Fails, Fool's Choice and documenting assignments.
- Notion-specific distinctions retained include genuine mutual purpose versus technique; Contrasting versus Apologizing; facts versus invented stories; understanding versus agreeing; controlling stories by retracing the Path to Action; consensus as a selective decision method; and recurring trust/commitment issues.
- The previous “42 concepts” claim was inaccurate. Do not reuse it.

## Visual quality and interaction checks

- Replaced repeated generic icons with a controlled system of **27 distinct concept-specific modern vector illustrations** in Safety, C.R.I.B., Path to Action, S.T.A.T.E., A.M.P.P. and decision making; additionally created the Middle Way, three-option, stress, law, Fool's Choice and accountability graphics.
- Functional browser run: 43 interactive concept controls passed at the stage tested, with no JavaScript console errors. Further controls added afterward require a final sweep before production release.
- Browser sizes covered: 320, 375, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px. Responsive tweaks were made for overflow.
- **Editorial approval remains outstanding.** Browser function, complete source coverage and objective legibility are not substitutes for the user's own visual approval.

## Release gate

Keep changes on `design/crucial-conversations-editorial-rebuild` and use a Vercel preview. Do not promote the branch to production until (1) content has passed source-coverage review, (2) all new pop-outs and icons pass complete visual and functional QA on current HEAD, and (3) the user approves the final design direction.
