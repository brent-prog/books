THE FOUNDATION

What's happening
in the conversation?

At a crucial moment we can avoid the conversation, handle it poorly or handle it well. The difference is whether relevant meaning can enter the shared pool.

SilenceWithhold meaning
DialogueFree flow of meaning
ViolenceForce meaning
MORE MEANING.
BETTER CHOICES.

A larger pool produces smarter decisions. Sharing meaning increases commitment—even when a leader makes the final choice.

The Fool's Choice

Believing that honesty and preserving the relationship are incompatible is a false choice. Skilled people find a way to do both.

SILENCE FAILS!In the best organizations, everyone holds everyone else accountable.
02
FIX YOUR MOTIVES

Start with heart.

Work on me first, us second. Ask what you really want before your motive slips into winning, punishing or keeping the peace.

Four questions that refocus the brain

01What do I really want for myself?
02What do I really want for others?
03What do I want for the relationship?
04How would I behave if I really wanted these results?

Turn either / or into AND

FALSE CHOICEHonesty
OR harmony

Two opposing options with undesirable consequences.

BETTER QUESTIONHonesty
AND respect

What could accomplish both outcomes?

03
RECOGNIZE DIALOGUE AT RISK

Make it safe.

When people stop contributing freely, look for silence or violence. The response is not to argue harder. It is to step out and restore safety.

SILENCE WITHHOLD MEANING

MASKING

Understating, sarcasm, sugarcoating.

AVOIDING

Steering around sensitive subjects.

WITHDRAWING

Exiting or disengaging.

VIOLENCE FORCE MEANING

CONTROLLING

Coercion and controlling the exchange.

LABELLING

Stereotyping or categorizing.

ATTACKING

Belittling and threatening.

05
UNDERSTAND YOUR REACTIONS

Master my stories.

Facts do not produce feelings directly. We first tell ourselves a story about what they mean. One set of facts can create infinite stories.

06
SPEAK PERSUASIVELY, NOT ABRASIVELY

S.T.A.T.E. my path.

Speak with confidence, humility and skill. Begin with verifiable facts, invite other interpretations and encourage disagreement.

07
EXPLORE OTHERS' PATHS

Understand before
you evaluate.

Make it safe for the other person to share their facts, stories and feelings. Listening is a skill; understanding does not require agreement.

08
TURN DIALOGUE INTO RESULTS

Move to action.

Decide HOW to decide. Then make assignments that turn ideas into action. Dialogue builds the shared meaning; decisions determine what happens next.
