THE FOUNDATION

Crucial Conversations.

Crucial Conversations can transform people and relationships. The Buddhist “middle way” is a higher middle way versus a compromise. The root cause of many human problems lies in how people behave when others disagree about high-stakes, emotional issues.

THREE CONDITIONS

When conversations become crucial

Opposing Opinions. Strong Emotions. High Stakes. These are the conditions that make a conversation crucial.

Dialogue creates an opportunity to move beyond disagreement rather than avoid it or manage it poorly.

TURN DIALOGUE INTO RESULTS

Move to action.

Decide HOW to decide. Then make assignments that turn ideas into action. Dialogue builds the shared meaning; decisions determine what happens next.

WHY IDEAS FAIL TO BECOME ACTION

01Unclear expectations about how decisions will be made
02Poor follow-through on the decisions that were made

DIALOGUE IS NOT DECISION MAKING. Make it clear who will decide, who will be involved and why—before assigning responsibilities.

Four ways to decide

Close the accountability loop

WHO?Give every responsibility a named owner. There is no vague “we.”
DOES WHAT?Specify deliverables; contrast what you do and don’t want; point to examples.
BY WHEN?Set a deadline. Without one, a goal is merely a direction.
FOLLOW UP?Agree how often, by what method, and how to verify completion.
