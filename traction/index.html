07 · Traction

90-day priorities. A meeting rhythm.

Two disciplines turn the vision into action: quarterly Rocks and a consistent Meeting Pulse.

Set the next quarter's Rocks

Cascade accountability

Company

Choose 3–7 priorities for the next 90 days.

Leadership

Assign one owner to every Rock; leaders carry 3–7.

Departments & people

Translate company priorities into departmental Rocks; individuals generally own 1–3.

Avoid four pitfalls: choosing the wrong Rocks, expecting instant mastery, allowing commitment to fizzle and taking on too many priorities.

Meeting Pulse

Same day. Same time. Same agenda. Start and end on time.
